**Description:**
Stop checking five sites for the same card.

Grailio watches the biggest card marketplaces and auction houses for you. Save a search once, like "Kucherov Shield BGS 9.5", and get a notification the moment a matching listing appears on:

• eBay
• Goldin
• Fanatics Collect
• MyCardPost
• Sotheby's
• Heritage (via your Want List emails)

Search the way you already do. Grailio uses the same search syntax as eBay: exact phrases, excluded words, either/or groups and wildcards. Filter by price, auction or Buy It Now, graded or raw, free shipping, and location.

Every alert links straight to the listing on the original site, so you bid and buy where you always have.
